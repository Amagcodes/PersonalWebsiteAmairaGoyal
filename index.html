01 / About

Curiosity rarely stays in one subject.

My interest in patterns started with Rubik's cubes and grew into robotics, statistical physics, and quantum information. I enjoy building things, then figuring out why they behave differently from the model in my head.

At Bombay Scottish School, Mahim, I study Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science and English in the ISC curriculum. Outside class, I work on research projects, program robots, mentor younger students, and enjoy playing the piano.

I'm particularly drawn to quantum communication and cryptography, computational physics, and using mathematical models to understand complicated systems.

02 / Research

Questions I'm working on.

I use simulations and mathematical models to study quantum communication and the behaviour of markets.

Published · National High School Journal of Science · 2026

A Statistical Physics Approach to Market Dynamics

Studied how information access, customer behaviour, and platform visibility affect competition between local and digital vendors. Built an agent-based model using Boltzmann probabilities, reinforcement and social influence.

In an ONDC-style simulation, increasing proximity weighting raised local vendor share from approximately 17% to 55%, while market concentration fell. These are simulation results.

Published in NHSJS · Presented at Sigma Xi (2026)

PythonStatistical physicsAgent-based modelling
Co-authored research · Under review at PennScience · 2026

Phase Transitions in Local and Digital Vendor Dominance

Extended the market model to examine local dominance, digital dominance and mixed-equilibrium phases. Used phase diagrams, Shannon entropy, market concentration and susceptibility to investigate tipping points and differences between agent-based and mean-field models.

Phase transitionsMean-field theoryComputational modelling
IIT Mandi · Research apprenticeship · 2026

Quantum key distribution & channel noise

During a six-month research apprenticeship, I've been studying quantum states, measurement, BB84 key distribution and security against eavesdropping. My work also explores entanglement-based QKD, channel noise and simulations of quantum communication.

BB84Quantum communicationNoise modelling
Fortytwo Labs · Internship · 2025

Post-quantum cryptography

Studied how Shor's and Grover's algorithms affect cryptographic security, including risks to RSA and ECC. Explored Kyber and Dilithium and wrote a paper with case studies on quantum threats to digital payments, communication and transaction integrity.

Quantum algorithmsCryptographyPayments security
ISC Physics · Computational project

Modelling the flight of a ball
